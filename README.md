# ComputerNotes

*Language*: English/[Chinese](README_Chinese.md)



During the undergraduate study period when majoring in Computer Science and Technology at university, I taught myself lots of useful and beneficial for lifetime computer knowledge through excellent online-courses on Bilibili. I sincerely express my thanks to these UPers’ selfless sharing. They are practitioners of the open-source spirit!

When teaching myself, I found after a few time I might forget the details and cautions, which is obviously not conducive to the persistence of knowledge. Therefore, after simply studying basic `Markdown` knowledge, I came up with an idea that I could use `Typora` to record the key self-learned knowledge and personal insights as `Markdown` notes. Then I carry forward the open-source spirit of the UPers who once taught me knowledge, and upload my notes to GitHub for open sourcing.

I have two purposes for doing this:

- To facilitate my own review and reference in the future.
- To help more beginners interested in computer science avoid some detours through my notes.

I will abide by the open-source spirit and strive to continuously improve my notes. That's all.
